Brand Tweet Generator
An AI-powered content engine designed to analyze a brand's unique personality and generate 10 perfectly tailored tweets. Whether the goal is to boost engagement, drive sales, or expand brand awareness, this tool ensures your social media voice remains consistent and impactful.

Features
Brand Voice Analysis: Uses AI to identify and summarize a brand’s tone (e.g., Bold, Minimalist, Premium, Confident).
Targeted Objectives: Choose from three specific campaign goals:
Engagement: Boost likes, replies, and shares.
Promotion: Drive sales and conversions.
Awareness: Expand reach and visibility.

Smart Generation: Delivers 10 distinct tweets based on industry category and product description.
Tone Matching: Generates a mix of witty, informative, promotional, and engaging content styles.
UI/UX Optimized:
Real-time loading states and progress indicators.
Copy-to-clipboard functionality for every tweet.
Character count tracking (Twitter 280-limit compliant).

Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js / Next.js (or Streamlit)
Styling: Tailwind CSS (Dark/Light Mode Support)
AI Engine: Google Gemini API / OpenAI GPT-4
Icons: Lucide React / FontAwesome

How It Works
Input Brand Details: Enter the brand name and industry (e.g., Nike - Sportswear).
Define Objective: Select what you want to achieve with the thread.
Describe Product: Provide a brief description of the product or service.
AI Analysis: The engine analyzes the brand's global persona.
Review & Copy: Browse 10 generated tweets, check character counts, and copy your favorites to X (Twitter).
